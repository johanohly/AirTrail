Aircraft photos appear in the flight details and the flight form when a flight has a registration and the integration is enabled. It is off by default.

## How it works

AirTrail looks up the registration on [Planespotters.net](https://www.planespotters.net/photo/api):

- **No API key needed**: the Planespotters photo API is free and keyless.
- **Latest photo of the airframe**: the photo can change over time, for example after a repaint, and disappears if it is removed from Planespotters.
- **Nothing is stored**: AirTrail keeps lookup results in memory for at most 24 hours, as Planespotters requires, and never in the database. Restarting the server clears them.
- **Attribution**: photos are always credited to their photographer, with a link to the photo page on Planespotters.net.

If Planespotters can't be reached or rate limits your instance, the photo shows a retry button. AirTrail holds back further lookups for as long as Planespotters asks.

## Setup

- In the app (admins only): **Settings → Integrations → Aircraft photos**.
- Or with an environment variable: set `INTEGRATIONS_AIRCRAFT_PHOTOS_ENABLED=true` and restart the server. This locks the setting in the app.

## Privacy

- AirTrail sends the registration of flights you view, and of any registration you type in the flight form, to Planespotters.
- Your browser loads photos directly from Planespotters' image servers, so Planespotters also sees your IP address. AirTrail never proxies or stores the images.
- Lookups are only available to signed-in users. They are turned off in public demo instances.
