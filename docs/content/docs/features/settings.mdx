Open **Settings** from the navigation dock. The first tabs apply to your own
account: **General**, **Preferences**, **Security**, **Appearance**, and
**Share**, plus **Import** and **Export** if your role allows them. Tabs below
those configure the whole instance and only appear for users with the matching
permissions.

This page covers **General**, **Preferences**, and **Appearance**. For the
**Security** tab, see [API keys and connected apps](/docs/features/api-keys).

## General

**Settings → General** shows your display name and your role (**Owner** for the
instance owner). Change your details here:

- **Username**: 3 to 20 characters. Letters, numbers, periods, underscores, and
  hyphens only. Usernames must be unique.
- **Display Name**: at least 3 characters.

Click **Save** to apply the changes. **Log out** ends your session in this
browser.

## Preferences

**Settings → Preferences** controls how AirTrail shows units, dates, and times.
Changes save as soon as you pick a value, and a preview below each section shows
the result. Preferences are stored on your account, so they follow you across
devices.

### Units

| Setting     | Options                                                                  | Default    |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| Distance    | Kilometers (km), Miles (mi), Nautical miles (nm)                         | Kilometers |
| Wind speed  | Knots (kt), Kilometers per hour (km/h), Miles per hour (mph), Meters per second (m/s) | Knots      |
| Temperature | Celsius (°C), Fahrenheit (°F)                                            | Celsius    |
| Pressure    | Hectopascals (hPa), Inches of mercury (inHg)                             | Hectopascals |

Wind speed, temperature, and pressure are used for airport weather.

### Date & time

| Setting        | Options                                                                 | Default            |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Time format    | Auto, 24-hour (`14:30`), 12-hour (`2:30 PM`)                            | Auto               |
| Date format    | Auto, ISO (`2026-04-26`), European (`26/04/2026`), US (`04/26/2026`)    | Auto               |
| Week starts on | Auto, Monday, Sunday                                                    | Auto               |
| Flight times   | Airport local time, UTC, My system timezone                             | Airport local time |

**Auto** follows your system settings.

**Flight times** controls which timezone departure and arrival times are shown
in:

- **Airport local time**: each time in its airport's timezone.
- **UTC**: all times in UTC.
- **My system timezone**: all times converted to your device's timezone.

### Presets

Click **Apply preset** to set all preferences at once. You can still change
individual settings afterwards.

| Preset   | Distance       | Wind speed | Temperature | Pressure | Time    | Date     | Week starts |
| -------- | -------------- | ---------- | ----------- | -------- | ------- | -------- | ----------- |
| Metric   | Kilometers     | km/h       | °C          | hPa      | 24-hour | European | Monday      |
| Imperial | Miles          | mph        | °F          | inHg     | 12-hour | US       | Sunday      |
| Aviation | Nautical miles | Knots      | °C          | hPa      | 24-hour | ISO      | Monday      |

All presets set **Flight times** to **Airport local time**.

## Appearance

**Settings → Appearance** sets the **Color Theme**:

- **System** follows your operating system's light or dark mode. This is the
  default.
- **Light**
- **Dark**

The theme is saved in your browser, not on your account. Set it separately on
each device.

Users who can configure instance map styles also see **Basemap Provider** on
this tab. That setting applies to everyone on the instance. See
[Basemaps](/docs/administration/basemaps).
